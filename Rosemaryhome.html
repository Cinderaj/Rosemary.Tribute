About

This webpage is dedicated to my late cat, Rosemary. (2009-2026)
Rosemary was the sweetest kitty and laughably silly sometimes.
She loved playing in boxes, with her toys, or with random objects like hairties and forks.
She was active and would follow you on walks outside including the woods.
She had a nose for discerning nonsence but was curious at the same time.
She loved belly rubs and attention in general even more so than food or treats.
She would paw at you or meow to be picked up and carried around on a shoulder.
She was my soulmate and would cuddle with me every night. I miss her dearly.
Therefore, this page is to celebrate her life and to share my love of this cat with the internet.
