Oscar

Oscar is Rosemary's brother and were adopted together in 2009.
Rosemary tolerated him.

Even when Oscar was young, he didn't play as much as Rosemary
and would often get embarassed and have to lick himself.

Sylvie

Sylvie is a kitten we found in the woodpile in 2026.
Her name is similar to Sylvian- "of the woods" for this reason.
Sylvie is rambunctious with boundless energy.
She must kill not only her toys, but everything in sight.
Like Rosemary when she was younger, Sylvie loves running around.
Unlike Rosemary, she parkours off furniture.
Rosemary didn't care for her but was too old at the time
to do much other than voice annoyance.
